Privacy Policy

Code de la Route Maroc · last updated November 6, 2026

1. Summary

Code de la Route Maroc (“the app”) is an offline study aid for the Moroccan driving theory test, published by Cool App Helpdesk.

  • The app collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app holds no Internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere. All signs, questions, images and videos are bundled inside the app.
  • Your learning progress and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no social SDKs in the app.
  • Links to official sources, sign credits and this policy open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • The Listen button uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. The app sends it only the text you asked it to read.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects nothing from this app. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage statistics, and it has no server of its own.

Because the app has no Internet permission, there is no channel through which it could transmit data. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

If you use Android’s backup service, your device may include the app’s local data in the backup of your device to your own Google account. That backup is controlled by Android and Google, not by the developer, who cannot access it. You can turn backups off in Android Settings → System → Backup.

4. Permissions used

The app requests no Android permissions at all. It does not ask for location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, notifications or network access.

PermissionReasonWhen asked

Opening a link hands the address to your browser through a standard Android intent; the app never loads the page itself. The Listen button asks the system for a text-to-speech engine in the same way.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app makes no network requests. It has no Internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or social SDK. Its only third-party libraries are general-purpose Android libraries (user interface, local database, media playback), and they run entirely on your device.

Links. The following open in your browser when you tap them:

  • Official Moroccan legal texts listed under More → About, hosted on official government websites (sgg.gov.ma and equipement.gov.ma);
  • Sign artwork sources listed under More → Sign artwork credits;
  • This privacy policy.

Once a page opens in your browser, that website’s own privacy policy applies. The app does not tell those sites who you are; it only passes the address you tapped.

Text-to-speech. The Listen button passes the displayed sign description to the speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Speech Services or your manufacturer’s engine). The app does not send that text to the developer. If your speech engine is set to download voices, it does so under its own settings and privacy policy.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose study aid for learner drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to review or delete beyond the local progress described in section 7.

7. Data retention and deletion

Nothing is stored on a server, so there is no server-side data to delete and no retention period to apply.

  • To delete all progress and settings: Android Settings → Apps → Code de la Route Maroc → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • To reset progress without clearing settings: More → Reset progress inside the app.
  • To remove a copy held by Android’s backup service, manage or delete your device backup in your Google account settings or turn backups off before uninstalling.

8. Security

Local data is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read the app’s private storage without root access. The app has no network permission, so your progress and settings cannot be transmitted by it. If Android’s backup service is enabled, the backup copy is encrypted by Google in transit and at rest under Google’s own security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
